# Human-Style Cover Letter Strategy

*For Remote Job Applications*

Attach this document + your resume to any LLM to generate cover letters and interest statements.

## How to Use This Document

1. Attach this document and your resume to any LLM (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, etc.).
2. Paste the Master Prompt from the last section and fill in the company/role details.
3. The LLM will extract your background only from the resume and write a human-style cover letter plus a short interest statement.

Do not invent personal stories or statistics. Keep the opening human and observational; move to resume evidence afterward.

## First Principles

- **Human observation first, credentials second.** The opening should name a real human reality or gap rather than listing skills or tools.
- **The company is the bridge.** The observation only works if it connects directly to something the company actually does, values, or is trying to solve for people.
- **Specificity prevents generic fluff.** Even an emotional hook must contain one checkable detail about this company.
- **Authenticity over drama.** Strong hooks feel observed and sincere. Sensitive topics only work when the connection is genuine and accurate.
- **Evidence follows the hook.** After gaining attention, move quickly into what you have actually done (from the resume).
- **Resume is the only source of candidate facts.** No invented experience or background.

## Strategy

### Research (before writing)

- What human problem or experience does the company's product, service, or culture actually touch?
- How do they talk about users, employees, or impact (mission page, recent posts, product language)?
- Is there one specific thing they do that shows they care about people, not just metrics?

### Hook structure (human version)

- **Sentence 1:** A short, concrete human observation or insight.
- **Sentence 2:** Direct link to what this company is doing about it.
- **Sentence 3 (optional):** Brief reason it matters to you or why you want to help.

### Then

- Transition into 1–2 resume-backed examples that show you can contribute.
- For remote roles: mention practical readiness (time zone overlap, written communication, independent delivery) only if true from the resume or stated by the candidate.
- Close with a clear next step.

## Recommended Letter Structure

1. **Paragraph 1 — Human hook (2–3 sentences).** Observational, non-technical, company-specific.
2. **Paragraph 2 — Resume evidence (1–2 strongest relevant achievements mapped to the job).**
3. **Paragraph 3 — Why this company + remote readiness (if applicable).**
4. **Close — Clear next step and thanks.**

**Target length: 250–350 words.**

## Example Human Opening (Illustrative Only)

> "People often feel the systems around them are built for efficiency more than understanding. When a company treats the person on the other side as more than a metric, that difference shows up in how the product works and how the team treats each other. That is what stood out about [Company]'s approach to [specific thing they do for users or employees]."

The next paragraph then moves into resume-backed contribution. Do not copy this example verbatim — generate a fresh one tied to the real company.

## Short "Interest in the Company" Answer

Keep to 4–5 sentences. Use the same human tone and one specific company signal.

**Structure:** specific human observation or company signal → why it resonates → what you can contribute (from resume) → brief close.

## Quick Reminders

- **Customize every letter.** The human opening must be specific to the company.
- **Keep the opening non-technical.** Skills and achievements belong in the body.
- **Numbers and concrete outcomes from the resume are still valuable** — just place them after the human hook.
- **For remote applications,** a short note on communication style or overlap hours helps, but only if accurate.
- **Sensitive topics require genuine, accurate connections.** When in doubt, choose a quieter human observation.
